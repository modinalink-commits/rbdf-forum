About the Forum

The Riazuddin Bazar Business Development Forum is a united and organised platform for the traders of Riazuddin Bazar. Established on 1 November 2026, the organisation’s main aim is to increase mutual cooperation among traders, protect their rights, and ensure the overall development of the market. We believe a strong business environment can be built only on unity, transparency and ethics. The Forum works to maintain good relations among traders, to promote fair business practices, and to establish discipline in market management. All activities of the organisation are run according to its established policies, and financial transactions are carried out with transparency and accountability. Through modern technology, online accounting and record-keeping have been put in place. Our goal is not only business development, but also to build an exemplary, united and respected community of traders. Unity is strength, and progress comes through development.

President’s Message

Md. Faruk
President

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu. The main purpose of founding the Riazuddin Bazar Business Development Forum is to build a strong platform of mutual unity, cooperation and progress among the traders of our beloved market. In today’s competitive business environment, coordination, transparency and mutual trust among us are essential if we are to survive and grow. The Forum will work to protect the rights of traders, maintain discipline in the market, and ensure a fair business environment. We want Riazuddin Bazar to be more than a commercial centre. We want it to become an example of unity, honesty and mutual respect. I call upon all members to perform their responsibilities properly, to abide by the decisions of the organisation, and to contribute together to the development of the market. Insha’Allah, our collective effort will turn this Forum into a successful and exemplary organisation. May Allah guide us all on the path of justice, honesty and unity. Wassalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah.

Secretary’s Message

Md. Minhaj Uddin
Secretary

Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuhu. The Riazuddin Bazar Business Development Forum is a collective effort of all of us traders. The aim of this organisation is the overall development of the market, the protection of traders’ rights, and a transparent and orderly business environment. We believe that unity is strength. The active participation of all members, mutual cooperation, and a respectful attitude towards the rules and policies of the organisation are the key to our success. As Secretary, my responsibility will be to run the organisation’s activities smoothly, to listen to the views of members, and to play an effective role in carrying out all decisions. Insha’Allah, we are committed to building a transparent, accountable and modern system of management. I seek the sincere cooperation of all members. Through our joint efforts, Insha’Allah, we will be able to turn Riazuddin Bazar into an exemplary and strong business community. Wassalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullah.
